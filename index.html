Game support & playability

Any .nes, .sfc or .smc cartridge can use an applicable mode. NES files need a valid iNES / NES 2.0 header. SNES copier headers are handled automatically.

SMW (original USA) retains the full gameplay randomizer. NES cartridges with CHR ROM get graphics-only effects. Castlevania (USA / Rev 1) has a dedicated graphics map. Super Mario All-Stars (original USA) gets palette changes plus SMB1, Lost Levels and SMB2 tile effects.

All other games use experimental data corruption. It can damage graphics, sound, game logic, saves, or prevent booting. Low intensity reduces changes but cannot guarantee playability. Keep your original ROM and use a fresh save. Stage, item, and boss randomization remains SMW-specific.
